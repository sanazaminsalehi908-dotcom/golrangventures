Cascade at golden hour

572 limestone steps (or the escalators inside, no judgment), sculptures on every terrace, Botero’s chubby cat at the bottom, and Ararat floating over the city at the top.

Friday · go up around 16:00

The Cascade is the big view: a giant stairway of terraces with sculptures, escalators inside if our legs give up, and Fernando Botero’s round cat at the bottom. From the top you see the whole city with Ararat behind it. Go up around 4pm on Friday so we catch golden hour before sunset.
