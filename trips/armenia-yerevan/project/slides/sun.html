Garni, the Sunday detour

Sunday is slow. Lahmajo brunch at Tun Lahmajo, then my suggestion: a half-day taxi trip to Garni Temple and Geghard Monastery, both about 40 minutes from the center. Back for a nap, pork shawarma at SAS, and a last look at Republic Square lit up at night.
