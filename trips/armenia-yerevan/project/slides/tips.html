Before we fly

Good to know

Money

Armenian dram (AMD). Cards work in most places; carry cash for markets.

Getting around

Yandex Go or the GG app for taxis. Cheap, and no haggling.

Weather

November is chilly: around 10°C by day, near freezing at night. Bring a real coat.

Book ahead

Lavash, In Vino and Amar fill up on weekend nights. Reserve early.

The party

Nameless events move around. Check their page for the date and address.

Three words

Barev = hello
Shnorhakalutyun = thank you
Kenats = cheers
